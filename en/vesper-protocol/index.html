Vesper Protocol: explore and hack an alien megastructure

Vesper Protocol is a space exploration and hacking game developed by the Flexarion studio. Drift through a 400-kilometre alien structure, read its code, and decide what to repair — or let die.

The world of Vesper Protocol

The year is 2231. Human probes have reached Tau-Heliade, an unstable red dwarf eleven light-years from Earth. Around it orbits the Vesper: a ring built by a vanished civilisation, covered in radiators and city-sized circuits. The structure still works. Its automatons, which the crew calls the Guardians, maintain systems whose purpose nobody understands.

You are Ilya Sorel, a critical-systems auditor aboard the relay station Anchor-7 (Ancre-7). When the star starts to collapse, the Vesper begins to emit a signal: a distress call, a warning, or a self-destruct sequence. You have a few orbital cycles to find out.

Gameplay

1. Zero-gravity exploration

Move with thrusts and anchor points through rooms with no up or down. Your suit manages two resources: oxygen and heat. Hacking a system generates heat; so does staying too close to an alien radiator. Every outing is a calculation.

2. Hacking through reverse engineering

Every door, lift and Guardian runs a program written in glyphs. Plug in your console, set breakpoints, inspect memory, then inject a patch. No reflex mini-game: you win by understanding what the code does.

3. Alien ternary logic

The aliens didn't count in binary. Their logic uses three states — negative, neutral, positive. Early puzzles teach you the rules step by step; the last ones ask you to write your own routines. Our ternary logic tutorial explains the basics, and the mini demo lets you try them in your browser.

4. Guardians that learn

Reuse the same exploit too often and the Guardians patch it. They remember your methods and deploy countermeasures, forcing you to vary your approach.

5. Repair or plunder

Every hacked system can be restored or stripped of its resources. Your choices change the state of the Vesper, the Guardians' behaviour and which of the four endings you reach.
