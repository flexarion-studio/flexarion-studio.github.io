Vesper Protocol mini demo: three ternary logic puzzles

Before plugging your console into the Vesper, practise on three alien locks. Each input takes three values: −1, 0 or +1. Set them so that every output reaches its target.

This demo needs JavaScript. In the meantime, read our ternary logic tutorial.

Click an input (or press Enter / Space) to cycle it; the up and down arrows raise or lower the value.

OutputValueTargetStatus

All three locks are open

You can now flip a value with NEG, combine two conditions with MIN and MAX, read a balanced ternary number and anticipate ADD's "wrap-around". That is exactly what the first hours of Vesper Protocol ask of you.

The rules in short

NEG(a)Flips a: +1 becomes −1, 0 stays 0.
MIN(a, b)The smaller value: ternary "AND".
MAX(a, b)The larger value: ternary "OR".
ADD(a, b)Addition modulo 3: +1 + +1 = −1, −1 + −1 = +1.
ABCBalanced ternary number: 9 × A + 3 × B + C.

Going further

This demo covers the basics of the puzzles in Vesper Protocol. The rules are explained in detail in the ternary logic tutorial. The full demo, with exploration, Guardians and oxygen management, is a free download on itch.io.
