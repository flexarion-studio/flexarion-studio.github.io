Flexarion, the sci-fi game studio behind Vesper Protocol

Flexarion makes hard science-fiction games where you understand machines before you beat them. Our first title, Vesper Protocol, sends you to hack the embedded systems of an alien megastructure orbiting a dying star.

What Flexarion does

Believable hard SF

Orbits, heat, oxygen, communication lag: at Flexarion, physics and computing are game rules, not set dressing. Every mechanic starts from a real scientific principle.

Hacking as exploration

Our players don't shoot aliens: they read their code. Reverse engineering, breakpoints, firmware patches… cybersecurity becomes an approachable game language.

A team of three in Provence

Flexarion is an independent studio founded in 2023 in Aix-en-Provence by three people who love science fiction, embedded systems and real-time rendering.

Vesper Protocol, our flagship game

In 2231, the relay station Ancre-7 picks up a signal from the Vesper, a 400-kilometre alien ring orbiting the dying red dwarf Tau-Héliade. You play Ilya Sorel, a critical-systems auditor: understand the machines that guard it before the star goes out.

  • Zero-gravity exploration with oxygen and heat management
  • Hacking puzzles in alien ternary logic (−1, 0, +1), inspired by real debugging
  • Alien Guardians that learn from your exploits and patch them

World, gameplay and fact sheet of Vesper Protocol

Flexarion at a glance

What is Flexarion?

Flexarion is the independent science-fiction game studio from Aix-en-Provence, France, that makes Vesper Protocol. It is a fictional studio, created for a communication and SEO teaching project in the BUT Informatique (computer science degree) at IUT d'Aix-Marseille: the website, demos and artwork are real; the studio team and its figures are invented. More about Flexarion.

Who made Vesper Protocol?

In the fiction, Vesper Protocol is developed by Flexarion's three founders: Léa Marchetti (creative direction and game design), Yanis Belkacem (engine and rendering) and Camille Roux (hacking design). In reality, the website, demos and game world were made by three computer science students: Adam MANKAI, Gabriel GOUIN and Mattéo YANNI.

Is there more than one “Flexarion”?

Yes. Other student groups from the same course also named their fictional studio Flexarion, with other games and other websites. This Flexarion is the one behind Vesper Protocol: its only official website is flexarion-studio.github.io, and its accounts are studio-flexarion on itch.io, flexarion-studio on GitHub, @flexarion.studio on Instagram and TikTok, and @flexarionstudio on YouTube.

Can I play it?

Yes: the ternary logic mini demo runs in your browser, and a free PC demo is available on itch.io. Early access is planned for 2027.
