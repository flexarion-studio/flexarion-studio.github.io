Flexarion, a science-fiction video game studio

Flexarion makes hard science-fiction games where you understand machines before you beat them. Our first title, Vesper Protocol, sends you to hack the embedded systems of an alien megastructure orbiting a dying star.

Discover Vesper Protocol Play the mini demo

What Flexarion does

Believable hard SF

Orbits, heat, oxygen, communication lag: at Flexarion, physics and computing are game rules, not set dressing. Every mechanic starts from a real scientific principle.

Hacking as exploration

Our players don't shoot aliens: they read their code. Reverse engineering, breakpoints, firmware patches… cybersecurity becomes an approachable game language.

A team of three in Provence

Flexarion is an independent studio founded in 2023 in Aix-en-Provence by three people who love science fiction, embedded systems and real-time rendering.
